GST Council - Latest Big Updates (2025-26)

  • 2-Rate Structure: Ab mainly 5% aur 18% slab - 12% aur 28% wale zyada items merge ho gaye.
  • Essential items 5% / 0%: Daily use, food, health, education se judi cheezein sasti.
  • E-commerce TCS: Online sale pe 1% TCS (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST) - seller ko ITC me milta hai.
  • E-Invoice: Turnover 5 Cr+ walo ke liye mandatory, B2B pe IRN + QR code.
  • IMS in GSTR-2B: Ab ITC pehle IMS me Accept / Reject / Pending karna hota hai, phir 2B me aata hai.

  • Stock, Invoice, E-way bill ka record rakho
